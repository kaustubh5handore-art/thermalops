THE PRODUCT

One temperature layer. Three P&Ls it protects.

ThermalOps is not a weather app. It's an operations layer that sits on top of FortyGuard's Temperature API and turns raw heat data into thresholds, alerts, and audit trails that finance, safety, and ops teams can act on.

Workforce & Employee Safety

Outdoor and floor-level heat-stress monitoring for construction, delivery, warehouse and field-service crews.

  • Site-level WBGT-style risk score, refreshed on FortyGuard's forecast cadence
  • Auto-triggered work/rest cycles per OSHA/ADOSH-style thresholds
  • Supervisor alerts over WhatsApp, SMS, and Slack — no app install for crews
  • Immutable incident log for compliance and insurance audits
−31%
modeled heat-incident rate, pilot cohort

Logistics & Cold Chain

Route-level thermal risk for last-mile fleets, reefer trucks, and temperature-sensitive freight.

  • Route thermal-exposure scoring before dispatch, not after a spoilage claim
  • Cold-chain breach prediction using ambient + corridor forecast data
  • Driver-safety heat alerts for cabin and loading-dock exposure
  • API hooks into TMS/WMS for automatic reroute or reschedule suggestions
4.2×
faster spoilage-risk detection vs. static thresholds

Data Centers & Facilities

Ambient-driven cooling risk for facilities where external heat load pushes PUE and hardware risk up.

  • External heat-load forecasting layered onto internal thermal telemetry
  • Early warning for cooling-capacity stress before it becomes downtime
  • Facility-level thermal risk scoring across multi-site portfolios
  • Exportable reports for sustainability and uptime-SLA reporting
18–72h
earlier warning vs. reactive monitoring alone

MARKET ANALYSIS

Heat risk is priced into insurance, payroll, and uptime — just not yet into software.

  • 01
    Occupational heat exposureOutdoor and industrial workforces across construction, logistics, and manufacturing face rising heat-stress days, and regulators in the Gulf, EU, and parts of the US are moving toward mandatory work-rest thresholds.
  • 02
    Cold-chain and freight lossTemperature-related spoilage and delivery delays are a recurring, budgeted loss line for logistics and last-mile operators — and one that's largely managed with static, non-hyperlocal thresholds today.
  • 03
    Data center thermal riskAs ambient temperatures climb, external heat load is an increasing contributor to cooling stress and uptime risk for facilities operators, on top of internal thermal telemetry they already track.
